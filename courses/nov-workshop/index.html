Begin the session

Section 01 · The Rewrite

Edit your profile like a searcher.

Open your profile now; this section happens inside it. Deloitte's marketplace research is blunt: the matching is only as good as the skills people write down. A partner reads your lines cold here.

Play · Findable or invisible?

Why this matters: Three lines from real-shaped profiles. Call each one the way a searcher would.

Grounded in: Activating the internal talent marketplace (Deloitte Insights)

Breakout · 3 min: Pair up at your table: swap devices or read lines aloud. Your partner marks each line findable or invisible. Rewrite one invisible line together before swapping back.

Section 02 · The Walk

Read the posting beside your profile.

Now the Portal, open on your device. Search by a skill you want to use more and pick one gig or opening that pulls at you, even slightly. This section is the craft of exploring it properly: reading it against your profile instead of window-shopping it.

Practice · The proper look

Why this matters: You found the one that pulls. Choose how you read it and what you keep from the reading.

Choose both steps

Breakout · 3 min: Tell your table partner which opening you picked and why it pulled. The partner asks the two questions: which of your lines matches it, and what is missing?

Section 03 · The Exit Ticket

Leave with a hand up or a barrier named.

Two good ways out of this room: interest expressed on something real, or the honest obstacle written down and handed to a navigator. Both move you. The only exit that goes nowhere is a vague intention to look again later.

The two-door exit

Expressing interest opens a conversation and signs nothing; you can step back at any stage. A named barrier, written and handed to a navigator, is a solvable problem. Both count as motion. A vague later is the only exit that goes nowhere.

In the room

The weekly session taught the open move; the clinic asks you to make one before you leave. In this room the cost of raising a hand is at its lowest: the navigator who can answer your barrier is standing here, and the manager conversation you are dreading can be rehearsed at your own table first.

Talk it through: What is the real barrier between you and the opening on your screen: timing, confidence, a skill gap, or a conversation you have not had? Who in this room could move it?

You found a gig you want, and you doubt your manager can spare you this term. Your exit?

Breakout · 3 min: Around the table, one sentence each: the opening you explored and which exit you are taking, hand up or barrier named. Navigators are listening for the barriers.
