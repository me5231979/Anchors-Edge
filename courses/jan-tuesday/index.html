Section 01 · The Preview

Opportunities that come looking.

The Transfer Portal is part of Goal 1's talent system, launching this year. You keep a skills profile; the Portal matches it against internal openings, stretch projects, and growth roles across Vanderbilt, and brings the fits to you.

How the matching works

The Portal reads skills and interests, never just titles. A named skill can match a role in a school you have never worked in; a named interest can surface a project before it is ever posted. What it cannot read is anything you left blank.

Quick check: what does the Portal use to surface an opportunity to you?

Breakout · 3 min: In pairs: what would you want the Portal to know about you that your job title never says? One pair shares.

On your own: Write down what you would want the Portal to know about you that your job title never says.

Section 02 · The Readiness

Findable is a choice.

Matching is only as good as what it can read. Two moves make you findable before launch: a skills profile that says what you can actually do, and interests named in writing. Build both here.

Practice · Build your findability

Why this matters: Two entries decide what the Portal can see. Choose how you write your skills and how you name your interest.

Choose both steps

Section 03 · The Culture

An internal move is a career path.

The Portal only works if people believe using it is safe. So hear the policy out loud: internal mobility is a normal, encouraged career path at Vanderbilt. Keeping talent inside the university is the point of Goal 1. Some old myths need retiring first.

Play · Myth or normal?

Why this matters: How people talk about internal moves decides whether anyone makes them. Call each one before the Portal launches into that culture.

Breakout · 3 min: In pairs: what have you heard, in any workplace, about what happens to people who apply internally? Myth or real, 90 seconds each way.

On your own: Write down one thing you have believed about applying internally. After this section, decide: myth or real?
